## Builder

`scripts/mumbai_city_builder.gd` — continuous road decks + per-segment collision
boxes (unchanged from the verified prototype), plus baked road-edge lines,
centre dashes on majors, and kerb + sidewalk strips. Ground plane with collision.

### Road surfaces (baked, node-efficient)

`StreetKit.add_road_deck(...)` draws the asphalt, lane-edge lines, centre dashes
and both sidewalks/kerbs into **one `ArrayMesh` per deck** (up to 4 surfaces),
instead of separate nodes per strip. Edge lines/dashes/kerbs/sidewalks are
visual-only — they add **no collision**, so they cannot raise the driving edge.

### Buildings (no OSM footprints available)

The Overpass clip is **highways-only** — there are no building footprints in
`mumbai_fort.osm.json` (0 building ways). Buildings are therefore generated by a
**documented frontage rule**, never arbitrarily inside the road network:

- Walk each road deck within `BUILD_RADIUS` (330 m) of the spawn; place buildings
  on both sides with their glazing facing the street.
- Sample every road corridor every ~8 m as `(x, road_half_width + sidewalk, z)`
  in a spatial hash (`GRID` 24 m).
- A candidate is accepted only if the **centre + 4 rotated footprint corners**,
  inflated by the maximum balcony/awning projection (1.4 m), each sit **≥ 0.5 m
  outside every road corridor** (`_all_points_clear`) — so buildings cannot block
  junctions or cross-streets — and the rotated world-AABB does not overlap an
  already-placed footprint (`_footprint_free`).
- Civilian collision is added only within `COLLIDE_RADIUS` (300 m); farther
  buildings are visual-only, capping `StaticBody3D` count.
- A shopfront-sign budget (`BuildingKit.signature_budget = 48`) limits signage.

Reuses `scripts/building_kit.gd` facades (windows, sills, cornices, roof tanks,
shutters, awnings, signage) — one merged mesh per building, cached materials.

Street trees at the sidewalk line are batched into `MultiMeshInstance3D`
(`StreetKit.flush_trees`).
